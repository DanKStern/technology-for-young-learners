# Technology for Young Learners

A Pre-K through grade 8 STEM curriculum spanning six strands — Forces & Motion, Matter & Energy, Life & Living Systems, Systems/Hierarchy & Nesting, Information & Communication, and Computing & Logic. Each strand progresses through four age bands (PreK-K, Grades 1-2, Grades 3-5, Grades 6-8), spiraling the same core ideas back around with more depth at each stage.

Written as a hands-on, conversation-driven curriculum — each lesson favors a simple activity and a question over a lecture.

A companion project to [becca-arcade](https://github.com/DanKStern/becca-arcade).

## Structure

- `00 General/` — curriculum outline, scope & sequence, and shared guidance
- `10 Forces & Motion/` through `60 Computing & Logic/` — one folder per strand, with a lesson file per age band
