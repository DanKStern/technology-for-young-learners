# Information & Communication — Band A (Pre-K–K, ages 3–5)

**Core idea:** people take turns talking and listening, and simple signals (a red light, a nod) can carry a message without words.

**Spiral note:** first pass. Pure social/sensory experience of sending and receiving; no "communication" vocabulary yet.

## Hands-on activities
- Practice taking turns talking in a group — one person talks, everyone else listens, then switch.
- Play with a toy traffic light or stoplight picture: red means stop, green means go.
- Charades — act out an idea with no words and see if someone else can guess it.
- Notice a baby sibling or pet "telling" you something without talking (crying, barking, pointing).

## Draft

### Taking Turns Talking

In a small group (even just the two of you plus a stuffed-animal "guest"), practice the rule: one person talks, everyone else listens, then it's someone else's turn. Make the rule visible, not just spoken, by passing a small object to whoever's turn it is. Ask her afterward: "What happens if two people talk at the same time?" and let her actually try it once, briefly, so she hears for herself that it doesn't work.

### Red Light, Green Light

Use a toy traffic light, a drawing of one, or just a held-up red vs. green card, and play a simple stop/go game — no words needed. Once the two signals are solid, ask her: "How did you know what to do without me saying stop or go?" Point out that the light was telling her something without talking at all — the whole idea of this lesson, just not named yet.

### Charades

Act out something simple without words — brushing teeth, a dog barking, eating ice cream — and let her guess, then switch roles. Afterward ask: "How did you understand me even though I didn't say anything?" Follow with the pet-or-sibling question if you have one on hand: "How does [pet/baby] tell you things without talking?" Collecting a few of these — the light, the charade, the pet — is the groundwork for naming "signals" and "messages" explicitly at [[Information & Communication - B Grades 1-2]].
